Draw. Review. Apply.

Create and edit geometry on a map. Every preview gives you a chance to review your work before applying it.

Your first shape

  1. Choose Line, Marker or Polygon, then press the green drawing button.
  2. Click the map to place points. A line needs at least two different points; a polygon needs at least three corners. A marker is saved with one click.
  3. For lines and polygons, choose Finish in the top bar, or press Enter with the map focused. Clicking the first corner also closes a polygon.

The top bar always names your current operation. Cancel discards its preview; Undo restores the last saved change. Undo becomes available again when you finish or cancel an operation.

If Finish is unavailable, read the message in the top bar. You may need another point or to correct crossing polygon edges. Backspace removes the last point while drawing.

Select the right shape

Click a shape. Shift+click adds or removes shapes from the selection; Shift+drag selects shapes intersecting a rectangle. Click empty space or use Deselect to clear the selection.

For overlapping shapes, click the overlap and open shapes here in the map toolbar. Choose a named shape, or use its checkbox to build a group. The first selected shape is the target for subtraction.

The map toolbar gives quick access to Edit, Copy and Delete. More tools opens the selection panel, grouped into Edit shape, Transform and Combine polygons. Unavailable actions have an explanation below them.

Drag a selected shape to move the group. Release saves that move; Escape during dragging restores its starting position. Undo restores a completed move. Drag elsewhere to pan the map.

Edit, review and apply

  1. Select one shape and choose Edit vertices.
  2. Drag a corner to move it. Click or drag a small midpoint to add a corner. Select a corner and use Remove vertex to delete it.
  3. Choose Save in the top bar to keep all edits as one undoable change, or Cancel to restore the original.

Shift+click selects several corners; Shift+drag selects them in a rectangle. Drag a selected corner to move the group. Polygon and line minimums still apply.

For multipart shapes, choose Part to edit first. For a polygon with holes, choose Ring to edit. Draw a hole creates an interior boundary; Delete hole removes the chosen interior ring.

With Snap to vertices & edges enabled, nearby corners and edges guide placement. Hold Alt to bypass snapping. Locked shapes remain available as references, but cannot be changed until unlocked. Demo locks reset on reload.

Choose a tool for your task

Operation settings appear at the top of the tool panel. Review the map preview and the message in the top bar before applying. Cancel keeps the originals.

Distances: buffer, offset and simplification use Mercator map-plane meters. These are not ground distances. Geometries near the poles or crossing the date line have additional limits; read the operation feedback.

Object colors and overlaps

Open Object colors & overlap. Enable Use object colors, select shapes, choose a color and drawing order, then Apply color & order. These object changes can be undone.

Overlap style can show ordinary transparency or alternating diagonal stripes. Use the object colors or choose a custom palette, width, opacity and direction. Add overlap example demonstrates three overlapping polygons.

During editing, overlap stripes are temporarily replaced by normal fills. Saving recalculates them; cancelling restores them. A message explains when standard colors are used because stripes cannot be displayed.

Try examples and keep your work

Under Your geometry, choose an example and press Add example. Shapes are added to your existing work and selected for the suggested task. Repeating an example creates fresh shapes. Undo removes the latest added batch.

Import GeoJSON adds supported shapes from a file up to 5 MB. Invalid imports leave existing work intact. Existing feature IDs cannot be imported twice.

Export GeoJSON downloads your committed shapes and properties. Apply an active preview before exporting it. The playground does not automatically save your work across reloads; export before leaving.

Keyboard and touch

  • With map focus: Enter finishes; Escape cancels or deselects; Delete removes the selection; Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redoes.
  • While editing vertices: Home on the map focuses the first corner. Page Up/Down changes corners; arrows move the focused corner. Shift+arrows moves 10 pixels. Space selects; Shift+Space toggles a group. Tab leaves the vertex control.
  • Touch or pen can move existing corners and add midpoints in vertex editing. Drag elsewhere to pan; a second contact cancels the current corner movement.

Whole-shape dragging and transformation handles currently require a mouse. Physical touch devices and browsers beyond the documented Chromium test scope still need qualification. A complete keyboard-only drawing workflow is not yet available.
