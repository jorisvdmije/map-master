ROTATE YOUR SELECTION

All selected shapes turn around the white centre marker. Positive angles turn clockwise. Apply saves the preview; Cancel keeps the originals.

OFFSET YOUR LINES

Positive is left of each line's direction; negative is right. 0 keeps the originals. Miter joins and perpendicular endpoints. Apply replaces the selected open lines; Undo restores them. Map-plane meters, not ground meters.

BUFFER YOUR SELECTION

Positive map-plane meters, not ground meters. Rounded corners and ends. Apply replaces each selected shape with its buffer; Undo restores the originals.

SIMPLIFY YOUR SELECTION

0 keeps every vertex. Increase to remove small details. These are map-plane meters, not ground meters. Invalid results cannot be saved. Neighbouring boundaries are not linked.

SCALE YOUR SELECTION

1 keeps the original size; 0.5 halves it; 2 doubles it. All selected shapes scale together around the white centre marker. Apply saves the preview; Cancel keeps the originals.
