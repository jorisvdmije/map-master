ROTATE YOUR SELECTION

Drag the ↻ handle to rotate around the white pivot. Drag ⊕ to move the pivot; it snaps to original vertices and the centre (Alt bypasses snapping). Moving the pivot recalculates the current angle from the original shapes. Hold Shift for 15° steps, or enter an exact angle. The dashed frame stays horizontal and vertical. Release to review; Apply saves the preview. Escape during a drag restores that drag; Cancel discards the whole rotation.

OFFSET YOUR LINES

Drag ↔ to change the offset or enter an exact distance. The ruler starts at the first line endpoint: positive is left, negative is right of its direction. Release to review; Apply replaces the selected lines. Escape during dragging restores that drag. Mercator meters, not ground meters.

BUFFER YOUR SELECTION

Drag ↔ outward to widen the buffer or enter an exact positive distance. The ruler starts at the original boundary. Release to review; Apply replaces each shape with its buffer. Escape during dragging restores that drag. Mercator meters, not ground meters.

SIMPLIFY YOUR SELECTION

Drag the slider or enter an exact tolerance. 0 keeps every vertex. Arrow keys make small adjustments; Home resets to 0. The faint solid outline shows the original; the dotted outline shows the preview. These are Mercator map-plane meters, not ground meters. Neighbouring boundaries are not linked.

SCALE YOUR SELECTION

Drag a corner to scale proportionally, or a side to change only width or height. Drag ⊕ to move the pivot, or fix a corner of the original bounds and drag the opposite handle. Moving the pivot recalculates the current factors from the originals. Alt bypasses pivot snapping. Factors: 1 = original, 0.5 = half, 2 = double. Release to review; Apply saves; Escape during dragging restores the starting size.

RESHAPE YOUR GEOMETRY

Drag a large point to move a corner. Click a small point between corners to add one.

Delete removes the selected corner. Save applies your changes; Cancel restores the original shape.
